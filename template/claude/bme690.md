
## BME690 (temperature, humidity, pressure, gas)

- Wiring, on the board's I2C connector (PH2.0 4-pin, 3.3 V): VCC to 3.3 V,
  GND, SDA to SDA (GPIO 45), SCL to SCL (GPIO 46), **CS to 3.3 V** (I2C mode;
  CS low at power-up selects SPI), **SDO to GND** (address 0x76) or 3.3 V
  (0x77). Read the connector's pin order off the silkscreen or the schematic.
  The boot's I2C scan then lists 0x76 or 0x77 next to 0x2f and 0x5d.
- It shares the bus `board.c` set up for the GT911 (`board_i2c_bus()`). Add
  devices to that bus; never create a second bus on GPIO 45/46. The
  i2c_master driver locks the bus per transfer, so several tasks can use it.
- `main/bme690.c/.h`: finds the sensor (0x76, then 0x77), reads it in forced
  mode every `PERIOD_MS` in its own task (priority 2), logs each reading,
  shows them on a card (`bme690_ui_create`, left middle) and keeps the last
  one (`bme690_latest()`). Without a sensor it logs a warning and stops; the
  rest of the app runs on.
- `main/bme69x.c`, `bme69x.h`, `bme69x_defs.h`: Bosch's BME69x Sensor API,
  v1.1.0 (github.com/boschsensortec/BME690_SensorAPI, BSD-3:
  `LICENSE.bme69x`), unchanged. Float output (`BME69X_USE_FPU`). Update it by
  copying the three files over, not by editing them.
- The API's write callback gets the registers interleaved (value, reg, value
  ...) after the first register: `wr()` sends `reg` and that buffer as one
  transfer.
- Gas: the resistance of the plate heated to `HEATER_C` for `HEATER_MS`. It
  is valid only when the status has both `GASM_VALID` and `HEAT_STAB`, and it
  drifts for minutes after power-up. An air-quality index (IAQ) needs Bosch's
  BSEC library, closed source and under its own licence: not included.
- The heater warms the sensor: the temperature reads a little high, more so
  with a shorter period. Lower the heater duty (a longer `PERIOD_MS`) or
  calibrate against a thermometer.
- The tick is 10 ms: `delay_us()` busy-waits delays under 10 ms and sleeps
  longer ones.
